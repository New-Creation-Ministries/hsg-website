# Home

Mode: Read

Audience: visitors and members, same pages. Job: identify Holy Spirit Generation and its leaders, then reach a lead-in destination. Content: [Home spec](../docs/features/home-landing-page/spec.md). Constraints: Spirit in Blue, Oswald and DM Sans, no motion, ADR 0002 navigation, Menu at 800px and below.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is a cobalt poster for the church name, then four lead-ins. The portrait is a reserved field. It refuses a pastor-name heading, a slogan, and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Ink ground, cobalt hero and sermon panel, pale yellow-green links and service times, a light testimony band. Oswald for display headings. DM Sans for body and navigation. [ADR 0003](../docs/adr/0003-spirit-in-blue-visual-direction.md).

STORY: The visitor reads the church name and who leads it, sees unpublished lines as text, distinguishes the one channel link, reads both Sunday services, and follows a destination label.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo only at the left, seven words at the right. Cobalt hero: church name and blurb, About under the blurb, silent HSG field to the right. No motion. The primary action is the text link at the end of a section.

FORM: Spirit in Blue, locked in `docs/features/home-landing-page/design-reference/02-spirit-in-blue.html`. Follow the spec; do not reopen the palette or the heading.
