- (event) Create brief initiated for a responsive church website covering home, events, testimonies, giving, and contact, with non-technical content updates and future regional extensibility.
- (event) Light external grounding noted Razorpay Web Standard Checkout, Google Calendar API, and YouTube embeds as plausible integration anchors; brief must decide simple embed/link versus managed workflows.
- (decision) Purpose is internal alignment for HSG church website launch, not investor/vendor pitch.
- (decision) Primary audiences are church members, newcomers local to Bangalore and outstation, and associated pastors from various locations.
- (decision) Brand identity: HSG stands for Holy Spirit Generation; tone should be youth-forward with a dark theme preferred by leadership.
- (decision) Content operations will be owned by church tech, media, and social media teams; current channels are Instagram and YouTube, with non-technical update capability required.
- (decision) Giving launch scope includes anonymous giving, donor receipts, offering categories, recurring giving, and finance-team exports; website will not sell products or services.
- (decision) Events should support both calendar sync and website-managed event pages; initial launch is India-only, with later extensibility to other regions.
- (decision) Technical direction includes Supabase backend, Vercel as hosting candidate, domain holyspiritgeneration.org, and an agent-friendly end-to-end testable/deployable delivery workflow.
- (event) Draft brief and addendum created; brief focuses on internal alignment while addendum preserves PRD and architecture handoff details.
