Healing prayer

Receive prayer on Sunday

HSG believes in healing through faith and the power of God. On Sundays, healing cards are created before the service so the Pastor and healing teams can pray for you.

We do not promise a specific healing outcome. Prayer is offered freely and is not connected to giving.

Sunday healing-card process
1

Arrive before service

Come 30–45 minutes early to the healing desk near the main entrance.

2

Create your healing card

A volunteer helps you write your prayer need on a healing card.

3

Receive prayer in service

The Pastor and healing teams pray for you during the Sunday worship service.
