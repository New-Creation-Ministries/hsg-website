🅿 Parking

On-site parking at the main campus. Overflow available at the community hall. North gate recommended on Sundays.

👶 Children's church

Programs for infants through teens during the 10:00 AM service. Check in at the children's desk 15 minutes before service.

♿ Accessibility

Wheelchair access at the main entrance. Reserved seating near the front. Contact us before your visit for additional support.

What to expect

  • Worship with contemporary and Spirit-led elements
  • Message from the Word of faith
  • Welcoming community — no dress code
  • About 90 minutes for the main service
