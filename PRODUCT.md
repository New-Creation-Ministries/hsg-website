# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors and members share one public site. They come to identify Holy Spirit Generation, its leader, what is happening, testimonies, messages, and Sunday services.

## Product Purpose

A public church website for Holy Spirit Generation in Bengaluru. Home’s heading is the church name. The introduction names Apostle Dr. P. S. Rambabu and Pastor Vinita Rambabu, and the page leads into Events, Watch, and Contact Us. Success is that those facts and destinations are readable without another page.

## Positioning

Holy Spirit Generation is a Word-based, Spirit-filled church in Bengaluru, founded and led by Apostle Dr. P. S. Rambabu and Pastor Vinita Rambabu.

## Operating Context

Content is published by deploying the repository. Visitors and members use the same pages. YouTube and Instagram stay external.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Routes: Home, About, Events, Watch, Contact Us, Give.
- Unpublished destinations show the destination name and “This page will be published here.”
- Home may store optional URLs and render links. No embeds, forms, or booking on Home.
- Playlist URLs, live highlights, testimony URLs, and a portrait are not yet supplied.
- Miracle-story wording is adapted from Rambo World Outreach and is not labeled as an HSG gathering.
- The street address stays off Home.
- Sunday services: Word Fest Service, English, 8–9am; Miracles and Healing Service, Multilingual, 9:30am onwards.
- No member accounts and no backend in this slice.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Holy Spirit Generation.
- Mark: `public/brand/hsg-logo.jpg`.
- Home visual direction: Spirit in Blue, accepted in `docs/adr/0003-spirit-in-blue-visual-direction.md`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Copy and routes in `docs/features/home-landing-page/spec.md` (approved).
- Interaction in `docs/features/home-landing-page/ux.md` and `docs/adr/0002-public-navigation-and-recovery.md`.
- No portrait file. Do not invent photos, playlist URLs, event details, or a street address.

## Product Principles

- Publish only facts the church has supplied.
- Visitors and members see the same pages.
- A missing URL is readable text, not a control.
- The destination’s own label is the way onward.
- The leader’s name and the church’s identity are available on Home.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Skip link, visible focus, and a Menu button that exposes the same seven destinations. Touch targets for Menu and navigation links are at least 44px. Text and accent links meet contrast on ink, cobalt, and the testimony band. No motion. Menu appears at 800px and below.
